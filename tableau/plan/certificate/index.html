    What this certificate is, plainly

    It certifies that a specific plan was run to completion: every step checked, on the honor system, the same way the work itself was done. The skills listed are the ones the plan actually contained. If someone wants to test whether the skills took, the Tableau kit and the certification kit are open to them too.
