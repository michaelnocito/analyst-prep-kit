# Tableau Kit — Roadmap

> ## 🧠 FIRST: GENERATIVE PROMPTING (opened 2026-07-27, Mike)
> **Nothing else in this kit's lessons changes until this is addressed.**
>
> Today this kit explains an idea, shows a worked example, then asks the learner to
> perform. At no point does it ask them to produce anything of their own first. That
> missing beat is why the material reads as informative but not engaging.
>
> **The four prompts, in ascending cost:**
>
> | Prompt | Where it goes in a Tableau Kit lesson | Evidence |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Prequestion** | Above the concept, one per lesson, aimed at the single idea the lesson exists to deliver | g = 0.66 on the asked idea |
> | **Self-explanation** | Between the worked example and the drill — the empty beat today | g = 0.55 |
> | **Imagination** | After the worked example, before the learner types | Grows as material gets harder |
> | **Drawing** | Optional, once per unit at most | g = 0.69, most expensive |
>
> **The rule that stops it backfiring:** explain first, prompt second. A learner asked
> to imagine something they do not yet understand is worse off. Hard cap of four or
> five prompts per lesson. Every prompt must work when read aloud by a phone.
>
