Time to stop for a minute.

    

    
    
      
  • Look out a window at the furthest thing you can find, and hold it for twenty seconds. Focusing close up for a long stretch is what makes eyes ache.
    • 
      
  • Blink hard, ten times. Staring at a screen cuts your blink rate, which is why they end up dry.
    • 
      
  • Stand up and walk out of the room. Even a lap of the kitchen counts.
    • 
      
  • Cold water on your face and wrists. It wakes you up faster than another coffee.
    • 
      
  • Roll your shoulders back and drop them. They have been up by your ears for the last stretch.
